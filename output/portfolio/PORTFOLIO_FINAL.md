## Project Proof — Short Version

I designed and implemented a reproducible audit analytics and expenditure-control system for internal-audit and financial-review workflows. It connects budget structures, expenses, procurement, audit tests, exceptions, risk levels, findings, reports, and immutable activity logging in one database-backed application.

The verified baseline includes:

- Django 5.2 LTS and PostgreSQL 16.
- 14 automated audit evaluators.
- 30 explicit RBAC permission codes across four standard roles.
- 353/353 automated tests passing in the documented local PostgreSQL run.
- 54 Ground-Truth validation tests, including exact two-way matching against 28 predefined Ground-Truth Control Exceptions.
- 100% coverage of the seeded synthetic training population: 43 expenses and 9 procurement cases.
- 264 exception rows/records in the full analytical output; these are records in the output, not 264 independent cases.
- SHA-256 verification of seven published evidence artifacts: 7/7 OK.

> The project is an independent training and demonstration system. It does not represent a real employer, client, commercial deployment, or measured business impact.
